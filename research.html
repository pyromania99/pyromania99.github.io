How the work unfolded

Two stages, separated by what I found in the estimator.

  1. Aug 2025 – Feb 2026

    Show that spinning pays

    Co-rotating, tangentially canted rotors and passive radial wings. Hover flights on an unmodified PX4 cascade at 8–10 rad/s. Result: wings carry ~22% of the weight. Published at ICUAS 2026.

  2. Sep 2026

    Find what limits the spin

    Moved to Isaac Sim + Pegasus with PX4 in the loop and ground truth beside the estimate. The ceiling was the estimator, not the controller. Two EKF2 corrections.

  3. Sep – Oct 2026

    Compensate the lag, fly it

    A feedback-linearising controller with adaptive lag compensation, running inside PX4. Simulated to 29.5 rad/s. First hardware flights: 8.6 rad/s, then ~15 rad/s.
