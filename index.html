2023 – 2026

Thesis

A quadrotor that spins on purpose. Treating the whole vehicle as one large, controlled propeller lets it draw on the efficiency of larger rotors, and aims at fault-tolerant flight at a lower spin rate.

  • ICUAS 2026 Best Paper Finalist
  • Wings carry 22% of the weight in hover
  • Controlled hover at ~15 rad/s on hardware

Next

Having explored what this platform offers, I want to bring the same mindset to the ground: a universal limb that can both locomote and manipulate, with the control structure designed around that mechanism.

Where I’m taking this →

Direction

Approach

I want to design the mechanism and its control together, so the platform and the controller shape each other. My starting point is nonlinear control with a classical mindset, which I want to apply to interpreting and designing RL-based controllers with a grounded understanding.
