PX4 estimator filter rate: above 6.5 rad/s, 3 of 3 flights were clean at the faster rate and 23 of 23 diverged at the stock rate.Simulation
Frame error in PX4’s attitude estimator, which turns feedback positive above 15.7 rad/s. After the fix, attitude error at 20 rad/s dropped from 68.2° to 0.76°. Hardware test above 15.7 rad/s still to do.Analysis Simulation
On a spinning body, a delay in the motors rotates the command direction by spin rate × delay; a motor lag also reduces its amplitude.Analysis
